OOT (Switch 2) - Keyboard and Mouse settings program for Lichii
==============================================================

This program installs the keyboard + mouse mod and lets you change keys and mouse settings
with clicks instead of editing a text file. It works with Lichii and its forks.
(You do not need the other zip: this program has the mod inside.)


HOW TO USE
----------
  1. Extract this zip (right-click > "Extract All..." > "Extract").
  2. Close Lichii completely.
  3. Double-click OoT-KeyboardMouse-Config.exe.
     If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC": "More info" > "Run anyway".
  4. Check the "Lichii data folder" at the top. If it says "(not found)", click "Change..." and pick it:
     in Lichii, File > Open Lichii Folders > Root Data Folder shows where it is
     (portable Lichii: pick the folder of lichii.exe).
  5. Choose your keys:
     - "Buttons" and "Movement" tabs: type or pick a key, or click "Press a key..." and press it
       (or click a mouse button in that small window).
     - "Mouse and camera" tab: camera mode, camera speed, invert up/down.
  6. Click Apply. Wait for "Done".
  7. Start the game, click its picture once and press Ctrl+F9 once (every launch).

When you change keys, the program also redraws the key icons inside the game for your keys.
For that it needs your game file (the .nup of OOT for Switch 2) once: it looks for it by itself
in Lichii's game folders, and asks you to pick it if it cannot find it.


CAMERA MODES
------------
  As the stick: the mouse stands in for the right stick, with the game's own curve and top speed.
  Native:       the mouse turns the camera itself, 1:1, like a PC game.
                Before playing, open OoT-MouseCapture.exe (in Lichii's data folder) and leave it open.
                Alt+Tab or closing it gives the normal mouse back.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
  - The program writes the same OoT-Keyboard-Mouse-Settings.ini that the manual way uses, so you can
    switch between the program and the file at any time.
  - Lichii must be closed when you click Apply (it rewrites its settings when it closes).
  - Other button-icon mods (PlayStation, Xbox, Steam Deck) are moved to the "mod-backups" folder,
    because only one can be active.
  - Your old Lichii settings are kept as config\qt-config.ini.pre-keyboard-mouse.
  - To put Lichii back the way it was (gamepad, no mod): close Lichii, open
    the program and click "Undo mod changes...".
  - The "Basic Controls" screen keeps the default key layout; the HUD and menu icons follow your keys.
